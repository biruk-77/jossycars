Electric Vehicles (EVs) Lowest Tax
Customs Duty (CBU) 15%
Excise Tax 0%
Sur-Tax 0%
VAT (CBU / CKD) 15% / 0%

*CBU = Completely Built Unit (imported full car), CKD = Completely Knocked Down (assembled locally). EVs enjoy massive tax exemptions in Ethiopia to promote green energy.

ICE (Benzene/Diesel Cars) Highest Tax
Customs Duty 35%
Excise Tax (by engine CC) 30% – 100%+
Sur-Tax 10%
VAT 15%
Social Welfare Levy 3%

Import taxes on gas cars compound exponentially. A traditional car with an engine capacity above 2.0L CC can reach cumulative import tax rates of 150% to 200% of the vehicle cost.
