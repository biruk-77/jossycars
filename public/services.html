Direct Import Ordering

We source, inspect, purchase, ship, and custom-clear your vehicle from international hubs. Submit your request, and our sales agents will generate custom import options from reliable suppliers overseas.

1

Consultation & Selection

Our sales agents walk you through available inventory or custom import catalog. We discuss specs, budget limits, tax statuses (EV vs Hybrid vs Gas), and logistics timeline.

2

Agreement & Deposit

A formal purchase agreement is drawn. For local vehicles, we coordinate immediate bank/cash transfer. For custom imports, we verify the inspection report and secure a down payment deposit.

3

Shipping & Port Clearance

We manage port transit from Dubai/China/Europe to Djibouti Port, clear handling charges, load onto carrier trucks, and coordinate customs clearance and inspection at Modjo dry port.

4

Final Inspection & Delivery

The vehicle is cleaned, fully detailed, and gets safety checked. We register the vehicle, issue license plates, and hand over the keys at our premium showroom in Addis Ababa.
